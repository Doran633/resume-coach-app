# v0.9.10 Post-Commit Authority Closure

Canonical Semantic Compilation remains the only authority for identity, owner,
type, eligibility and Fact/Claim lineage.  After Canonical Project Projection
has completed, later services may validate, remove confirmed pollution, and
perform deterministic formatting with aligned provenance rows.  They may not
create a project, refill intro or role from a Fact, infer a missing field
attachment, rename a project, merge projects, or alter owner/type.

Legacy callers keep their raw-input recovery behavior.  This boundary applies
only to the Canonical production path and does not change Prompt, LLM, DOCX or
the public payload schema.
